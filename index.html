Server set-up

The cool part. I offer various decoration styles: custom dividers, embeds, and more. I set rules, add bots, adjust roles, create channels, set onboarding, whatever you want. Although it may seem skippable, I strongly suggest not cutting down on this aspect. A great, related, readable and cool decoration for your channels, roles, embeds and images is what defines if members engage or ignore. Eccentric or simple; it is your choice.

  • Roles and permissionsClear hierarchy, staff roles, member roles and the permission check. Cool & Safe at the same time.
  • Channels and categoriesA structure that makes sense to new members and scales with the community, and of course, looks-first.
  • DecorationCustom dividers, embeds, icons and images to match the style requested.
  • Rules and onboardingWelcome flow, rules and self-assign roles so people know where to go.
  • Bots and integrationThe bots your community needs, connected to your game when possible.
